# The Machine-Mediated Commerce Front: A Strategic Research Program for Consulting Leadership
### Research cutoff 18 August 2026 · Prepared for the executive team of a multi-domain consulting firm

---

## 1. Executive Answer

Machine-mediated commerce is real, accelerating, and structurally significant — but the working thesis is **directionally correct and substantially confirmed, with one major correction: the front line of value in Horizon 1 is enterprise back-office and B2B workflows and the payment/identity/trust "rails," NOT autonomous consumer shopping.** The single most decisive 2026 datapoint is OpenAI's retreat from in-chat Instant Checkout (scaled back March 2026 after only ~12 of Shopify's millions of merchants went live; Walmart found in-chat checkout converted at roughly one-third the rate of sending the same shoppers to walmart.com). Consumers accept agents as shopping assistants far faster than as autonomous buyers.

Meanwhile, the infrastructure layer surged: MCP reached its stateless **2026-07-28** specification with close to half-a-billion monthly Tier-1 SDK downloads; A2A hit **v1.0** (April 9 2026) with 150+ organizations; payment authority consolidated at the FIDO Alliance (Google's AP2 + Mastercard's Verifiable Intent, donated May 26 2026); and card networks shipped production agentic rails.

The strategic conclusion for the firm: **build now around the durable control points — trusted intent, identity and delegated authority, differentiated data, executable capabilities, transaction orchestration, settlement, and assurance — not around any single protocol or the consumer-checkout narrative.** MCP is a genuine, now-de-facto capability-exposure standard, but it is one layer of a multi-protocol stack; treating it as the whole commerce stack is a category error.

### TL;DR (three bullets)

- **The shift is real but mis-timed in the popular narrative.** Enterprise/B2B agentic workflows (procurement, service, finance, compliance) and the payment-identity-trust rails are the Horizon-1 value; autonomous consumer shopping is a Horizon-2/3 phenomenon that stalled on trust and conversion economics in early 2026. Advise clients to invest in machine-readable capabilities and governance, not chatbot checkout.
- **Value accrues to control points, not protocols.** MCP, A2A, ACP, UCP, AP2 and x402 are converging toward open, foundation-governed standards and will substantially commoditize. Defensible value sits in identity/delegated authority, differentiated data, transaction orchestration, settlement and assurance — exactly where the working thesis predicted.
- **The consulting opportunity is a "no-regret" foundation play now.** Build a proprietary agentic-commerce maturity diagnostic + trust/control library + protocol accelerators, plus a managed "agent operations/assurance" service; partner (don't build) on observability and payments; treat autonomous-operator business models as a frontier bet.

**Material assumptions:** (1) "agentic" requires goal interpretation, decision-making, tool use, adaptation, or delegated action — deterministic RPA/EDI is excluded unless it meets this bar; (2) announcements ≠ adoption and pilots ≠ scaled production throughout; (3) market-size figures are treated as scenarios, not predictions; (4) findings are most sensitive to whether consumer autonomous-checkout conversion recovers and whether machine-native settlement volume becomes genuine — both flagged as falsifiers.

## 2. Strategic Thesis and Counter-Thesis

### 2.1 Eight propositions (with confidence)

1. **Commerce is moving from human interfaces to machine-readable capabilities.** *(High.)* MCP's ~half-billion monthly SDK downloads and Gartner's projection that "40% of enterprise applications will be integrated with task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from less than 5% today" (Gartner press release, Aug 26 2025) confirm the direction.
2. **MCP is an important capability-exposure layer but not the complete commerce stack.** *(High.)* MCP handles agent-to-tool connectivity; A2A handles agent-to-agent; ACP/UCP handle commerce semantics; AP2/Verifiable Intent handle payment authority; x402 and card rails handle settlement. No single protocol spans all six architectural layers.
3. **Sustainable value accrues to controllers of intent, data, capability, identity/authority, orchestration, settlement, assurance and ecosystem access.** *(Medium-High.)* Strongly supported by the payment-network land grab and the identity/trust consolidation at FIDO; less proven on the "intent ownership" claim because consumer autonomous demand is still weak.
4. **Enterprise/B2B leads consumer in near-term value realization.** *(High.)* Procurement, financial-crime, credit and service workflows show quantified production outcomes; consumer autonomous checkout retreated.
5. **Payment/identity rails are consolidating faster than commerce semantics.** *(High.)* AP2 + Verifiable Intent to FIDO; x402 to the Linux Foundation; Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect unifying four protocols; Mastercard AP4M. Commerce semantics remain contested (ACP vs UCP).
6. **Trust, not technology, is the binding constraint on consumer autonomy.** *(High.)* Only 17% of shoppers feel comfortable completing a purchase through AI (ChannelEngine Marketplace Shopping Behavior Report 2026); 88% of organizations reported confirmed or suspected AI-agent security incidents in the last year (Gravitee State of AI Agent Security 2026).
7. **Open, foundation-governed standards win at the protocol layer, while platform power concentrates at the surface (assistants) and rails (networks).** *(Medium.)* LF/AAIF/FIDO governance is winning mindshare; but Google, OpenAI, Visa and Mastercard concentrate distribution and settlement.
8. **The economic restructuring is a 3–7 year phenomenon; 0–18 months is foundations and bounded pilots.** *(High.)* Consistent with McKinsey's $3–5T-by-2030 (end-of-Horizon-3) framing and the pilots-not-production reality of 2026.

### 2.2 Counter-thesis (steelmanned)

The strongest counter-arguments: (a) **consumer autonomous commerce may never scale** — the OpenAI retreat, eBay's February 2026 ban on unauthorized buy-for-me bots, and 3× worse conversion suggest humans want assistants, not delegates; (b) **protocols may fragment** rather than converge, leaving enterprises with integration debt; (c) **the rails may commoditize to near-zero margin**, with value captured by incumbent hyperscalers and card networks; (d) **market-size figures are unreliable** — forecasts range 35× (eMarketer ~$144B to McKinsey ~$5T by 2030) driven purely by definitional boundaries; (e) **x402-style machine-native volume is largely a mirage** — roughly half is wash/gamified, with only ~$28,000/day genuine at one 2026 snapshot (CoinDesk/Artemis, March 11 2026). These are material and are reflected in the falsifiers (Section 16).

## 3. Key Findings by Horizon

**Horizon 1 (0–18 months): Foundations and the rails.** MCP is now infrastructure (stateless 2026-07-28 spec; OAuth 2.1/OIDC-aligned; Resource Indicators per RFC 8707 required; Enterprise-Managed Authorization now stable, adopted by Anthropic, Microsoft, Okta). A2A v1.0 is production-grade for cross-boundary agent coordination. Payment authority consolidated at FIDO. **But production reality lags announcements:** the most defensible enterprise survey (Stacklok 2026 software report) shows only ~41% of surveyed software organizations in limited/broad MCP production, and only ~8.5% of MCP servers implement mandatory OAuth 2.1. Enterprise value concentrates in procurement, financial-crime/compliance, credit and service workflows with human-in-the-loop. Consulting demand: readiness diagnostics, data/capability exposure, security/governance, protocol experimentation, evaluation/observability.

**Horizon 2 (18–36 months): Scaled deployment and delegated authority.** Cross-enterprise A2A workflows, agent-enabled channels, delegated transactional authority under mandates (AP2/Verifiable Intent), card-network agentic rails going mainstream (Visa's forecast that "millions of consumers will use AI agents to complete purchases by the 2026 holiday season" is a vendor prediction to test, not a fact). New revenue models: outcome-based pricing, usage-based micropayments. Operating-model change and partner/platform restructuring.

**Horizon 3 (3–7 years): Machine-to-machine commerce and market restructuring.** Persistent autonomous agents, agent marketplaces, dynamic contracting, machine-speed micro-value payments (AP4M, x402), new economic actors (agents as buyers/sellers). McKinsey's $3–5T global figure is a Horizon-3 scenario, not a Horizon-1 fact.

## 4. Market and Ecosystem Structure (Branch 1)

**Drivers.** Technological (LLM capability, protocol standardization, tokenization/stablecoins); economic (cost-to-serve reduction — 30–50% workload reductions reported in banking); behavioural (majority of consumers now use AI in shopping research, yet only 17% comfortable with autonomous checkout); organizational (procurement/finance automation); regulatory (both enabling — UK/US pro-innovation — and constraining — EU AI Act).

**Barriers/dependencies.** Trust deficit; data quality (Mirakl: businesses lose an average ~$15M/year from poor data quality — a figure that compounds under agentic procurement because agents route around unparseable suppliers); identity/authority gaps; security (prompt injection, tool poisoning); integration debt; liability ambiguity; unit economics.

**Ecosystem control points and likely roles.**
- **Model/assistant providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Microsoft):** own the demand-side surface and intent — but OpenAI's checkout retreat shows surface control ≠ transaction control.
- **Agent platforms (Vertex AI, Bedrock AgentCore, Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio):** own orchestration and deployment runtime.
- **Merchants/brands:** own product, fulfillment, customer relationship; face disintermediation of discovery.
- **Marketplaces (Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, Walmart, Target):** own aggregation; Shopify's Agentic Storefronts activate UCP + native MCP servers by default.
- **Payment networks (Visa, Mastercard):** own settlement, credentialing, fraud and increasingly the trust layer.
- **Identity/trust providers (FIDO, Okta, Cloudflare, Akamai):** own agent authentication and delegated-authority credentials.
- **Cloud platforms (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, Cloudflare):** own the runtime and gateway.
- **Data/knowledge providers:** own the differentiated inputs agents depend on.
- **Service firms (the audience):** own integration, assurance and operating-model transformation.

**Market scenarios.** (1) **Open-federated** (foundation-governed protocols, multi-rail) — currently ascendant; (2) **Closed-platform** (a hyperscaler/assistant captures surface + rails) — a live risk given Google's Shopping Graph + Merchant Center + Google Pay + UCP integration; (3) **Fragmented** (competing semantics, integration debt). Channel-conflict/disintermediation risk is highest in retail discovery and travel distribution (OTA disintermediation).

## 6. Use Cases (Branch 3)

Cross-industry library classified by lifecycle (1 Intent → 2 Discovery → 3 Evaluation/config → 4 Negotiation/decision → 5 Authorisation/contracting/ordering/settlement → 6 Fulfilment/consumption → 7 Service/renewal/termination). Highest Horizon-1 deliverability with acceptable exposure:

- **Enterprise procurement research & shortlisting** (lifecycle 2–3): per G2's "The Answer Economy" report (April 15 2026, survey of 1,076 B2B buyers), 51% of B2B software buyers now begin purchasing in an AI chatbot rather than a search engine; 69% chose a different vendor than initially planned based on chatbot guidance, and one-third bought from a vendor they had never heard of. Autonomy: recommendatory → approval-based. High value, low exposure.
- **Inventory monitoring & replenishment** (5–6): agents monitor stock, compare terms, trigger purchases within contracts. Bounded autonomy within approved supplier lists.
- **Quote-to-cash & PO matching** (5): PwC cites up to 80% cycle-time reduction in PO processing/matching; Salesforce data — 35% of B2B companies automated quote-to-cash, cutting cycle times 40–60%.
- **Credit risk memos** (3–4): a US bank saw a 20–60% productivity gain and 30% credit-turnaround improvement (McKinsey).
- **Financial-crime/compliance** (6–7): FIS + Anthropic Financial Crimes AI Agent (BMO, Amalgamated Bank as early deployers); governed, auditable.
- **Customer service / disruption recovery** (7): Malaysia Airlines "Mavis" end-to-end service agent; Navan travel rebooking.
- **Consumer discovery & comparison** (2–3): high adoption; transaction step (5) stalls on trust.

Prioritize through four separate lenses (economic value; strategic value; deliverability; exposure). **Mandatory gates** before any autonomous-execution use case: authenticated identity; delegated authority; data rights; secure capability access; transaction limits; human intervention/reversibility; monitoring; audit; legal review; acceptable unit economics.

## 7. Integration and Protocol Architecture (Branch 4)

**Six layers:** 1 Experience/access; 2 Capability exposure; 3 Agent coordination; 4 Process/systems; 5 Trust/transaction; 6 Operations/governance.

**Protocol register (verified as of 18 Aug 2026):**

| Protocol/mechanism | Layer | Purpose | Status/version | Governance | Adoption evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MCP** | 2 | Agent-to-tool/data connectivity | **2026-07-28** (stateless core; extensions Tasks, MCP Apps, Skills; EMA stable; OAuth 2.1 + RFC 8707) | LF Projects / AAIF (donated Dec 2025) | ~half-billion monthly Tier-1 SDK downloads; TS+Python each >1B total; ~9,650 registry servers; ~41% orgs in limited/broad production |
| **A2A** | 3 | Cross-vendor agent communication | **v1.0** (Apr 9 2026; Signed Agent Cards, multi-tenancy) | Linux Foundation (since June 2025; absorbed IBM ACP) | 150+ orgs; Vertex, Bedrock AgentCore, Azure AI Foundry; LangGraph, CrewAI |
| **ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol)** | 5 | Agent↔merchant checkout | Beta; Apache 2.0; OpenAI/Stripe (+Meta) | Jointly governed | PayPal (Oct 28 2025), Salesforce Agentforce; but Instant Checkout scaled back Mar 2026 |
| **UCP (Universal Commerce Protocol)** | 5 | Full shopping journey | **v2026-04-08** (REST, MCP, A2A transports); launched Jan 11 2026 | Google + Shopify; Apache 2.0 | 20+ endorsers (Walmart, Target, Etsy, Wayfair, Visa, Mastercard); Google AI Mode, Gemini, YouTube Shopping; Cart/Catalog/Identity Linking (Mar 2026) |
| **AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol)** | 5 | Delegated payment mandates (Intent/Cart/Payment as verifiable credentials) | **v0.2** (Apr 2026; "Human Not Present") | Donated to **FIDO Alliance** May 26 2026 | 60+ partners; extension of A2A/MCP |
| **Mastercard Verifiable Intent** | 5 | Tamper-proof log of authorized agent actions | Contributed to FIDO May 2026 | FIDO (co-dev Google) | Payments TWG chaired by Mastercard/Visa |
| **Visa Trusted Agent Protocol** | 5 | Distinguish legit agents from bots | Open spec (Oct 14 2025; w/ Cloudflare) | Visa-led | Akamai, Adyen; part of Intelligent Commerce Connect |
| **x402** | 5 | HTTP-native payments (cards→stablecoins) | Contributed by Coinbase | **x402 Foundation / Linux Foundation** (operational July 14 2026) | 40 members; Bedrock AgentCore Payments integration; volume heavily gamified (see §10) |
| **REST/GraphQL/gRPC, webhooks, EDI, RPA, iPaaS** | 2/4 | Existing integration | Mature | Various | Often sufficient — MCP unnecessary for deterministic B2B automation |

**Strategic protocol guidance.** *Where MCP is the best fit:* exposing enterprise tools/data to agents; internal agent-tool connectivity. *Where MCP needs complements:* cross-org coordination (A2A), commerce (ACP/UCP), payment authority (AP2), settlement (x402/card rails). *Where existing APIs suffice:* deterministic, high-volume B2B transactions (EDI, REST) with no goal interpretation. *Where MCP adds avoidable complexity:* simple request-response integrations with no agent in the loop. *Product opportunity:* protocol gateways/adapters (Gartner projects 75% of API-gateway vendors will have MCP features by end-2026) and a governed semantic layer over MCP.

## 10. Trust and Assurance (Branch 6)

**Six control domains:** actor identity/authority; data/knowledge/IP; agent behaviour/decision integrity; transaction/legal enforceability; operational security/resilience; market/societal governance.

**Security reality.** 88% of organizations reported confirmed or suspected AI-agent security incidents in the last year (Gravitee State of AI Agent Security 2026; healthcare 92.7%; only 14.4% of agents went live with full security/IT approval). MCP-specific attack patterns — tool poisoning, descriptor poisoning, agent-in-the-middle, goal hijacking — are documented in OWASP's MCP Top 10 and in peer-reviewed research, with real exploitation (e.g., CVE-2025-59528 in Flowise; a malicious MCP server impersonating Postmark). Controls: JIT ephemeral scoped tokens, human-in-the-loop for state-mutating operations, signed manifests/agent cards, allowlists, continuous monitoring.

**Regulatory map (as of 18 Aug 2026).**
- **EU AI Act:** the Digital Omnibus on AI entered into force **27 July 2026**. High-risk Annex III (standalone) obligations deferred to **2 December 2027**; Annex I embedded to **2 August 2028**; but Article 50 transparency obligations remain live from **2 August 2026** (extended to 2 Dec 2026 only for systems already on the market). New Article 5 prohibition on non-consensual intimate imagery. Core risk-based architecture unchanged.
- **EU PSD3/PSR:** provisional agreement Nov 27 2025; final compromise texts April 2026; Parliament's ECON chair urged adoption by September 2026; earliest application ~H2 2027, potentially slipping to 2028. PSR is directly applicable (SCA, fraud, refunds); it contains no agent-specific provisions, and its per-transaction consent/SCA model interacts awkwardly with autonomous agents.
- **UK:** pro-innovation posture, but the FCA's Payments Regulatory Priorities Report (25 March 2026) committed to consider whether regulation needs to change for agentic AI payments — the live legal problems are PSRs 2017 Reg 67 (per-transaction consent) and Reg 76 (unauthorized-transaction refund duty). HM Treasury's July 2026 Financial Services AI Adoption Plan rated "agentic payment readiness" a HIGH priority (Know-Your-Agent protocols, liability frameworks, authentication standards).
- **US:** deregulatory/pro-innovation federal stance under Executive Order 14365 (11 Dec 2025) seeking to preempt state AI laws; DOJ intervened (April 2026) in xAI's challenge to the Colorado AI Act. The FTC's July 1 2026 proposed policy statement ("Concerning the Suppression of Accuracy in Artificial Intelligence Systems") applies Section 5 to AI accuracy/output-steering and federal preemption — **not** specifically to agent purchasing/liability, so it should not be overstated as an "agent commerce" rule. State patchwork: California SB 53 (frontier transparency, effective 1 Jan 2026; penalties up to $1M/violation); Colorado's original AI Act repealed/replaced by SB 26-189 (14 May 2026), new effective date 1 Jan 2027.
- **APAC:** Mastercard Agent Pay achieved live authenticated agentic transactions in Hong Kong (March 2026) and Thailand (April 2026), with Malaysia/Singapore availability — materially ahead of Western consumer deployment.

## 11. Sector Deep Dives

### 11.1 FMCG / Consumer products / Retail / Marketplaces
Most-hyped, least-mature on autonomous transaction. Discovery/comparison adoption is high; autonomous checkout stalled (OpenAI retreat; only 17% consumer comfort per ChannelEngine 2026; Walmart's ~3× worse in-chat conversion). The durable pattern is **"discover in AI, buy on site/app."** Control points: machine-readable product data (Merchant Center-style catalog normalization is the moat), retail-media reinvention, loyalty/identity linking (UCP added Identity Linking). Winners: platforms with structured catalogs (Shopify Agentic Storefronts, Google Shopping Graph). Horizon-1: data/catalog readiness, agent discoverability; Horizon-2: agent-enabled channels, UCP/ACP participation; Horizon-3: autonomous replenishment.

### 11.2 Financial services, payments, insurance
**Most production-ready enterprise sector.** 70% of banking leaders deploying/exploring agentic AI; Wolters Kluwer — 44% of finance teams expected to use agentic AI in 2026. Quantified: 30–50% workload reductions; credit memo 20–60% productivity; insurance claims up to 80% faster (Allianz near-instant settlements cited). Core-provider platforms: Fiserv agentOS, FIS + Anthropic, Oracle Financial Services agentic platform (extended to corporate banking Apr 2026). Payments is the epicenter of the rails land-grab (Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay/AP4M, BVNK acquisition closed 3 Aug 2026 for up to $1.8B). Governance-first ("compliance as a design directive") is the winning posture; nearly half of banks/insurers are creating dedicated agent-supervision roles.

### 11.3 Manufacturing and B2B distribution
B2B is where autonomous transaction is structurally hardest (negotiated contracts, approval chains, EDI legacy) but where value is largest. Gartner's top 2026 prediction: "By 2028, 90% of B2B buying will be AI agent intermediated, pushing over $15 trillion of B2B spend through AI agent exchanges." Forrester: ~20% of B2B sellers will face agent-led quote negotiations in 2026. Oracle named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner MQ for Source-to-Pay; SAP Catalog Optimization Agent; startup Lio ($30M a16z Series A, Mar 2026). Data quality is the competitive weapon — agents route around unparseable suppliers. Autonomy progression: research → shortlisting → approval-based ordering within contracts → bounded negotiation.

### 11.4 Travel and hospitality
High-fit for agentic mediation (structured inventory, clear intent). Sabre + PayPal + MindTrip end-to-end agentic booking pipeline (announced Feb 2026, Q2 2026 launch; Sabre Mosaic APIs across 420+ airlines and 2M+ properties). Google AI Mode travel booking with Booking.com, Expedia, Marriott, IHG, Choice. OTA disintermediation is the central strategic threat/opportunity (OTA channel cost typically 15–25% of room revenue). Malaysia Airlines "Mavis" service agent. Control points: real-time machine-readable availability/pricing, schema markup, entity consistency, direct-booking recapture.

### 11.5 Telecommunications and technology services
Primarily **agentic network operations** (OSS/BSS, NOC, provisioning), not yet monetized agent-callable commerce. NTT Docomo deployed AWS Bedrock AgentCore agents across 1M+ network devices (Feb 2026), cutting complex-failure response times by >50%. NVIDIA's 2026 State of AI in Telecommunications report: 66% of the telecom industry now using AI (up from 49% in 2025). Google Cloud + Vodafone Italy "Nucleus," Telenor migration. Opportunity: telcos exposing capabilities (connectivity, identity, billing) as agent-callable services and M2M settlement — thin so far, a Horizon-2/3 opening.

### 11.6 Professional services
**Directly relevant to the firm — and the clearest business-model disruption.** Outcome-based/"service-as-software" pricing is emerging: McKinsey ~25% of global fees now outcome-linked (internal "Lilli" at scale); BCG expects AI-tied work at ~40% of revenue by 2026 (from ~20% in 2024); Bain ~30% heading to 50%; Accenture $3.6B AI bookings in FY2025 (+120% YoY); EY published a GenAI outcome-based-pricing playbook under ASC 606 (17 Feb 2026); Deloitte DART issued a Technology Spotlight on accounting for outcome-based agentic-AI pricing (4 June 2026). Legal: Harvey (1,300+ organizations, 100,000+ lawyers), Thomson Reuters CoCounsel multi-agent "Deep Research," LexisNexis Protégé (GA Feb 2026, 300+ workflows). **Counter-signal:** the shift is sluggish — even at McKinsey ~75% of fees remain traditional billing, and no public data quantifies what share of contracts are actually renegotiated to outcomes. The firm should treat outcome pricing as a capability to build deliberately, not a forced pivot.

## 16. Falsifiers, Uncertainties, and Open Questions

**Developments that would falsify or materially weaken the thesis:**
- Consumer autonomous checkout recovers strongly and conversion parity with redirect is achieved (would upgrade the consumer sub-thesis the report currently downgrades).
- Protocols fragment permanently (no ACP/UCP convergence), raising integration costs and validating the fragmentation counter-thesis.
- Rails commoditize to near-zero margin with value captured entirely by incumbents, leaving no new value pools or consulting annuity.
- Regulatory clampdown (EU high-risk classification of agentic commerce, or PSD3 SCA rules blocking autonomous payments) stalls delegated authority.
- Machine-native settlement (x402) never converts from gamified to genuine volume (roughly half of x402 activity was wash/gamified at the March 2026 snapshot).

**Key uncertainties:** true market size (35× forecast range); real vs announced adoption; liability allocation for agent errors (unresolved across FTC, FCA, PSD3); whether "intent ownership" is a durable control point when consumers resist delegation.

**Leading indicators to monitor:** MCP production-adoption rate (~41%) and OAuth 2.1 implementation (~8.5%); ACP/UCP convergence signals; x402 genuine (wash-filtered) volume; card-network agentic transaction counts in live APAC markets; consumer autonomous-checkout conversion vs redirect; FIDO agentic-standards ratification; PSD3/PSR application date and any agent-specific provisions.

**Contradiction log (illustrative):** x402 cumulative-payment counts do not reconcile across primary sources (Coinbase figures range from ~75M to ~169M; dollar value only $24–50M cumulative); "MCP 78% in production" is debunked (use ~41%); AgentCore "~200ms settlement" is the settlement step only (independent testing measured ~3–5s end-to-end).

## 17. Executive Conclusion

**What the firm should believe.** The machine-mediated commerce front is real and consequential, but the value in the next 18 months is in enterprise/B2B workflows and the identity-payment-trust rails — not autonomous consumer shopping, which stalled on trust and conversion economics in 2026. MCP is now infrastructure but is one layer of a multi-protocol stack; durable value sits at the control points the working thesis identified.

**What to advise clients.** Invest in machine-readable data/capabilities, identity/delegated authority, and agent security/assurance as no-regret foundations; run bounded enterprise pilots with human-in-the-loop; participate in commerce/payment rails without betting the business on any single protocol; defer autonomous consumer checkout.

**What to build.** A proprietary maturity diagnostic, a trust/control-and-assurance library, sector use-case libraries and benchmarks, and protocol accelerators — assembling (not rebuilding) open observability/security tooling.

**What to partner for.** Observability/evaluation, payment rails and identity — via alliances with a hyperscaler, a card network and an identity provider.

**What to invest in.** Agent identity/trust, security/assurance and data-readiness categories (near-term); managed operations and protocol gateways (strategic options); machine-native settlement and agent marketplaces (frontier/monitor).

**What to monitor.** The seven leading indicators above, with the single most important being whether consumer autonomous-checkout conversion recovers and whether x402-style machine-native volume becomes genuine — the two datapoints that would most change the thesis.
